# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Course demonstrators and travellers exploring a travel-planning prototype. The home page should quickly communicate travel discovery and provide an inviting way into travel search and itinerary planning.

## Product Purpose

A classroom demonstration of a connected travel-planning experience for discovering products, organizing an itinerary, and coordinating plans with simulated bookings.

## Positioning

The prototype brings travel discovery, itinerary planning, and booking coordination into one demonstrable journey. This is a course project, not an official Ctrip product or service.

## Operating Context

The application is a Vue 3 and Vite web prototype. Its standalone home page leads into existing travel channels and the trip-planning assistant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product, price, availability, booking, and service-result data are simulated locally.
- The prototype does not connect to Ctrip, suppliers, payment, or real booking systems.
- Confirmed bookings are read-only to the assistant. Plans without a confirmed simulated booking must remain clearly unbooked.
- The home page is a distinct entry surface and must preserve access to the existing channels and itinerary assistant.

## Brand Commitments

- Ctrip's travel theme and recognizable blue travel-service identity are the reference for this classroom demonstration.
- The requested home-page direction combines Apple's restrained, photography-led presentation with Ctrip's travel-discovery subject and service-oriented entry points.
- The visual reference does not imply official affiliation.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing simulated channels, itinerary workflows, and travel assets are in the project source.
- No real supplier inventory, transaction evidence, or commercial claims are available; do not imply otherwise.

## Product Principles

- Make travel discovery and the next useful action immediately legible.
- Keep product facts, plans, and simulated bookings distinct.
- Use real interface controls for demonstrated actions; do not imply a live transaction.
